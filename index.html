THE WAVE ROOM FIELD GUIDE

A little editing goes a long way.

  1. Bring in a sound. Open an audio file or use the original Daylight study demo. Files stay on this device.
  2. Pick your good part. Drag on the waveform, or type the start and end in seconds. Click once for a cursor; use Select all for the whole clip.
  3. Shape it. Trim keeps your selection. Delete removes it and joins the rest. Silence preserves timing. Fades cover the selected region. Normalize sets the selected peak to −1 dB; it is not loudness normalization.
  4. Listen, then save. Playback starts at the selection or cursor. Loop repeats the selected region, or from the cursor to the end. Download a 16-bit PCM WAV of the full clip or the selection.

Good to know

One mono or stereo clip at a time. Maximum 25 MB encoded file, 3 minutes, and 64 MiB decoded audio. Your browser determines supported import formats and the decoded sample rate. WAV export only. No recording, multitrack mixing, plugins, or Audacity project support.

This is a tab-only session. Reloading closes your work. Undo keeps up to 40 edits within a 128 MiB history budget, so older steps may be discarded. Export your audio to keep it. Playback volume affects listening only, not the downloaded file. Start with low device volume.

Keyboard

Space play / pause · Esc stop · Ctrl/⌘ Z undo · Ctrl/⌘ Shift Z redo · Ctrl/⌘ A select all. Shortcuts leave text fields and buttons alone.
